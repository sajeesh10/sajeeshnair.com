# Publishing a post

Full routine: [skills/publish-blog-post/SKILL.md](skills/publish-blog-post/SKILL.md). The essentials:

1. He writes essays in **Obsidian on his Mac**. Vault: `/Users/sajeeshn/Obsidian-Notes/Cerebro`. Essays live in `Areas/Startups-Product -Engineering-Leadership/ramblings/` (note the space before the hyphen in the folder name).
2. He says "publish <note name>" or pastes a path. Reading the note needs his Mac: start a **Remote Control** session on "MacBook-Pro" in `~/Obsidian-Notes/Cerebro`, and have it read the note, edit the repo, build and push. Pass his own message along; the Mac's safety check once refused to push on a relayed request until he said "publish" himself.
3. Copy the body **verbatim**. Drop stray non-essay bits and say so: a leading `Date: …` line, trailing `---` or `-----`, pasted file names.
4. Date: a **random earlier day of the current month, never in the future**, even when several posts go out the same day.
5. Tags in `topic:`, comma-separated; say which you picked.
6. He sometimes asks for small things inside the request (link to an earlier post, fix bullet nesting). Do exactly that and nothing else.
7. Pull, build, check light and dark, push, confirm the live URL.
8. Reply with the live URL and a **numbered** list of optional suggestions (typos, grammar, brand casing like RunWhen, headings, closing line). Don't apply them.
9. He answers with numbers ("fix 1, 3, skip 4", partial picks like "3 - RunWhen only") or **"apply all"**. Apply exactly that, push again, then offer to apply the same edits to his Obsidian note.

Renames and republishes: keep the slug/URL, date and tags unless told otherwise.
