## The short version

- **Site:** https://sajeeshnair.com, plain HTML/CSS generated by `build.py` from `content/` into `public/`. Repo `sajeesh10/sajeeshnair.com`, branch `main`. Every push to `main` auto-deploys on Netlify (site "sajeeshnair", publish dir `public`, no build command). `public/` is committed.
- **Always `git pull origin main` first.** Several sessions push to main.
- **Who he is on the site:** an engineering leader with systems roots. Leadership writing is front and centre, the old performance-engineering posts live on `/archive/`.
- **Publishing:** he writes in Obsidian on his Mac and says "publish <note>". Copy his words verbatim, pick tags, pick a date (random earlier day of the current month, never in the future), push, then send numbered suggestions separately. Apply only the ones he picks.
- **Never edit his words** unless he picks a suggestion. No auto-generated diagrams or banners.
- **No secrets in this folder.** Nothing here holds tokens or credentials.
