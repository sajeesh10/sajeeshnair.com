Hi, I'm Sajeesh.

I spent the first half of my career making systems fast and reliable at scale, and the second half making teams fast and reliable at scale. Turns out the overlap is uncanny.

A couple of decades in, I'm still a systems engineer at heart. I just apply it to organisations now: find the bottleneck, keep talent density high, and design the teams the way you want the product to look, because thanks to Conway, it will anyway.

Off the clock: books, coffee, behavioural psychology, risk and probabilistic thinking, and lately health and fitness. The body is a system too, and it has strong opinions about sleep.
